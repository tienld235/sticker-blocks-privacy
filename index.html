Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Short version: Sticker Blocks does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your stars, scores, sticker collection and albums stay on your own device. We never see them. The only data leaving your phone is what Google AdMob needs in order to show ads.

What the app stores

Saved on your device only, never sent anywhere:

  • Your stars, best score, and daily challenge score and medals
  • Sticker packs waiting to be opened and your progress toward the next pack
  • The stickers you own (including HOLO versions), which ones are stuck in your albums, completed albums and unlocked board themes
  • The date of your last daily gift and your streak
  • Sound effects, music and vibration on/off, and your chosen language

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no server and no backup — if you uninstall, the progress is gone for good.

Motion sensor

HOLO stickers shimmer when you tilt your phone. The app reads the motion sensor only while it is open, just to move the shine. These readings are never stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app draws a picture of your stickers or score and hands it to your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app to share with, and the picture goes only where you choose.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To do that, Google may collect and process:

  • An advertising identifier (a resettable ID, not your name)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device type, operating system version, and basic app usage signals

We configure AdMob with a maximum ad content rating of G (suitable for everyone). Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to keep playing after the board fills up, double your pack progress after a game, or double the daily gift. Google's own privacy policy explains what they do with this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy — you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Data security

Sticker Blocks has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your data anywhere. Your progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings; either one deletes it immediately. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Children

All content is rated G. The game is not directed to children under 13; its target audience on Google Play is 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Permissions

The app requests only what is required to display ads (internet access, network state and the advertising ID) and to vibrate. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with an updated date at the top.
